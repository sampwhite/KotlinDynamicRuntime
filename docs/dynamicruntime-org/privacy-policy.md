# Privacy policy

*Effective November 9, 2026*

dynamicruntime.org is a public demonstration of KotlinDynamicRuntime, an open-source runtime for building forms and
workflows. It is run by Gyassa Software ([gyassa.com](https://gyassa.com)) ("we"). It is a place to try the software,
not a service to keep anything in: we keep as little about you as the demo needs to work, we use it for nothing else,
and **we wipe everything on the site every month** (sometimes sooner, without notice).

## What we keep, and why

**When you sign in.** You sign in with Google or with a code we email to you.

- With Google, we receive your Google account's **email address**, whether Google has **verified** it, your **name**,
  and, for a Google Workspace account, its **domain**. We do not receive your Google password, contacts, files,
  calendar, or anything else in your Google account, and we never ask Google for more.
- With an emailed code, we receive the email address you type.

We keep your email address and name so you can sign in again, and so the pages you use can show who you are. That is
all we use them for.

**What you enter.** Forms you fill in, clients you create, files you upload, and anything else you type into the demo
are kept so the demo can show them back to you and to the other people in the same demo client. **Do not enter real
personal, confidential, or sensitive information.** Everything is visible to the site's operators, may be seen by other
visitors in a shared demo client, and is wiped on the schedule above.

**Cookies.** We set a cookie that keeps you signed in, and after you sign in, one that identifies your browser to your
account. Both are needed for the site to work. We set no advertising or tracking cookies, and we run no analytics.
Your browser may also keep a few page settings (such as which users you recently switched to) in its own storage,
which never leaves your browser.

**Logs.** The application's log records the requests made to it, with passwords, codes, and keys blanked out, so we can
find and fix problems. It does not record your IP address. Every month we delete the logs that are more than a month
old, so no log is kept for more than about two months.

## Who else handles it

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for their own use. A few services handle it on our behalf,
only to run the site:

- **Linode (Akamai)** hosts the server, its database, and the files you upload, in the United States.
- **Mailgun** sends the emails the site sends: sign-in codes and invitations.
- **Google** confirms who you are when you sign in with Google.
- **Anthropic**, only if you use the **workflow assistant** in a client you created. The assistant is off until that
  client's administrator switches it on, after a notice saying what is sent. When you use it, your messages to it and
  the parts of your client's configuration it works on are sent to Anthropic to produce its answers. Nothing is sent
  to Anthropic if you never use the assistant.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

## Deleting your information

- **It is deleted for you** when the site is wiped, every month.
- **Ask us** at [admin@gyassa.com](mailto:admin@gyassa.com), and we will delete everything we keep about you before
  then: your sign-in, your users in any demo client you joined, and what you entered.
- **You can also remove your sign-up yourself**: on your **Profile** page, under **Your users**, **Remove**. That ends
  your sign-up on the site; once nothing else is left under your address, the address and its sign-in are deleted
  too. What you entered in demo clients stays until the site is wiped, or until you ask us.

Removing your access from your Google account settings stops dynamicruntime.org from signing you in with Google; it
does not by itself delete what is already on the site.

## Children

The site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep information about them.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. Since the site is wiped
regularly, a change never applies to information kept under an earlier version for long.
